# What real things are made of

Know the object before modelling or coding it: its parts, how many, rough sizes, what moves and how. When unsure of a
specific real model (a Ford F-150, a Spitfire), look up a reference picture/spec first (WebSearch / Browser) - then build.
Sizes are typical, in metres. y is up; the front faces +Z in the Forge studio (-Z in three.js/Godot cameras by default).

## Vehicles

- **Car** (4.2-4.8 L x 1.8 W x 1.45 H): body with hood, cabin (greenhouse: windscreen raked 25-30 deg, side windows, rear
  window), boot/trunk; **4 wheels on 2 axles** (wheelbase 2.6-2.9, track 1.55, wheel radius 0.32, tyre width 0.22),
  wheel arches around each; 2 or 4 doors (hinged at the front edge), 2 side mirrors, 2 headlights + 2 tail lights,
  grille, front + rear bumpers, number plates, exhaust at the rear, steering wheel on one side, seats. Moving parts:
  wheels spin about the axle, front wheels steer, doors/hood/trunk hinge, suspension compresses.
- **Pickup / SUV**: same + taller (1.8-1.9 H), bigger wheels (r 0.38-0.42), pickup has an open bed behind the cab.
- **Truck / lorry**: cab over or behind the front axle, 3+ axles (6-18 wheels, rear axles often doubled), trailer on a
  fifth wheel. **Bus**: 12 L, 2-3 axles, doors on the kerb side, rows of windows.
- **Motorcycle** (2.1 L): 2 wheels in line, front fork steers (rake 25 deg), tank, seat, engine between the wheels, exhaust,
  handlebars with mirrors, single headlight. Leans into turns. **Bicycle** (1.75 L): 2 wheels (r 0.34), diamond frame
  (top tube, down tube, seat tube, chain/seat stays), fork, handlebars, saddle, cranks + pedals + chain on the RIGHT side.
- **Tank**: hull, 2 tracks each over 5-7 road wheels + drive sprocket + idler, turret on a ring rotating 360 deg, gun
  elevating -10..+20 deg. **Train**: locomotive + cars on bogies (2 bogies per car, 2 axles per bogie), on 1.435 m rails.
- **Plane** (airliner): fuselage, 2 main wings with ailerons + flaps, 2 (or 4) engines under the wings, horizontal
  tailplane with elevators, vertical fin with rudder, tricycle landing gear (nose + 2 mains), cockpit windows at the nose.
  Light aircraft: 1 propeller at the nose, high or low wing, wingspan ~11 m. **Helicopter**: main rotor 4-5 blades on a
  mast, tail boom, tail rotor (vertical, on the side), skids or wheels.
- **Boat**: hull (keel, bow, stern), deck, cabin/wheelhouse, rudder + propeller at the stern; sailboat: mast, boom, sails.
  **Spaceship** (fiction): hull, cockpit, engines/thrusters at the rear, wings or fins optional, landing legs.

## Buildings and props

- **House**: foundation, walls (2.5-3 m per storey), pitched roof (30-45 deg) with eaves overhang 0.3-0.5, front door
  (0.9 x 2.1), windows (sill at 0.9 m, head at 2.1), chimney, steps, gutters. **Door**: frame, leaf, hinges on one side,
  handle at 1.0 m on the other. **Window**: frame, sill, panes/mullions.
- **Stairs**: rise 0.17-0.19, run 0.25-0.30 per step, handrail at 0.9. **Table**: top at 0.75, 4 legs inset; **chair**:
  seat at 0.45, back to 0.9, 4 legs; **bed**: 2.0 x 1.4, mattress at 0.5; **lamp post**: 4-6 m.
- **Chest**: box base, lid hinged at the back (opens to ~105 deg), metal bands/straps, lock plate at the front, feet.
  **Barrel**: bulged staves, 2-4 hoops, flat heads. **Crate**: planks + frame edges. **Torch**: handle + wrapped head + flame.
- **Tree**: trunk (taper, root flare), branches splitting at Y forks getting thinner, canopy clusters; conifer = cone of
  layered tiers. **Rock**: irregular, flatter than tall, sinks into the ground. **Fence**: posts every 2-2.5 m + 2-3 rails.
- **Castle**: curtain walls with crenellations (merlons + gaps), towers at corners, gatehouse with portcullis, keep.
  **Bridge**: deck, railings, supports/arches or cables. **Well**: round stone wall, roof on 2 posts, rope + bucket + crank.

## Creatures

- **Quadruped mammal**: torso (chest deeper than hips), neck, head, 4 legs, tail. Front legs: shoulder blade -> elbow
  (bends back) -> wrist -> paw. Hind legs: hip -> knee (bends forward) -> hock/ankle (bends back) -> foot. Digitigrade
  (dog, cat, fox, horse on hooves) walk on toes; plantigrade (bear, human, raccoon) on the whole foot. Walk = lateral
  sequence (LH, LF, RH, RF); trot = diagonal pairs; gallop = suspended phase. 2 eyes, 2 ears, 1 nose, mouth.
- **Horse**: long legs (half the height), deep chest, long neck + head, mane, tail, hooves; 1.6 m at the withers.
  **Dog/wolf**: 0.6-0.8 m at the shoulder; **cat**: 0.25; **bear**: 1.0-1.5 at the shoulder hump; **deer**: antlers (males).
- **Human / biped**: 7.5 heads tall; shoulders 2 heads wide; elbows at the waist, wrists at the crotch; knees halfway from
  hips to floor; feet 1 head long; 2 arms (shoulder, elbow, wrist, 5 fingers), 2 legs (hip, knee, ankle, foot). Walk:
  opposite arm swings with each leg, pelvis drops toward the swing leg, head stays level.
- **Bird**: 2 legs (knee hidden, the visible "knee" is the ankle - bends back), 2 wings (3 segments, primaries at the tip),
  tail feathers, beak, no teeth. **Fish**: body, dorsal fin, tail (caudal) fin, pectoral fins behind the gills, eyes on the
  sides; swims by S-wave down the body. **Insect**: 3 segments (head, thorax, abdomen), 6 legs on the thorax, 2 antennae,
  0-4 wings. **Spider**: 2 segments, 8 legs, 8 eyes. **Snake**: no limbs, moves by lateral undulation.
- **Dragon** (fiction): quadruped body + 2 wings (bat-like membrane between long finger bones, attached at the shoulders),
  long neck and tail, horns, scales; wyvern = 2 legs + 2 wings.

## Weapons and tools

- **Sword**: blade (fuller groove down the middle, edge bevels, tip), guard/crossguard, grip (wrapped), pommel; one-handed
  0.9 m, longsword 1.1-1.3. **Axe**: haft + head (bit + poll). **Bow**: limbs + riser/grip + string; arrow: shaft, fletching
  (3 vanes), nock, head. **Shield**: face, boss, rim, straps behind.
- **Gun**: barrel, receiver, trigger + guard, grip, stock (long guns), magazine, sights; muzzle flash and ejected casings
  on the right side. Pistol 0.2 m, rifle 1.0 m.

## How they behave (for gameplay)

- Doors swing on hinges (or slide); drawers slide; lids hinge at the back; levers rotate about one end; wheels roll without
  slipping (`omega = v / r`); pendulums swing `T = 2*pi*sqrt(L/g)`; boats float and bob; smoke rises; sparks fall; water
  flows downhill; fire spreads to flammables; glass shatters; wood splinters; metal clangs.
